# Dimensional Rift Portal

The six titanium armor sections follow a continuous elliptical carrier. Dark blue inset panels sit at each flank. The thin teal light trace and recessed violet lip surround a removable, UV-mapped vortex surface. The static vortex is original artwork generated by the source script. P4 can replace it with the animated shader.

## Placement

All values below use glTF coordinates in metres. Place the identity root at the wall or ceiling anchor and rotate local +Z into the room along the host normal. X and Y span the opening. The rim extends 0.12 m beyond the aperture on both principal axes. Overall width and height are 1.19 × 1.59 m. The mounting flange sits at Z = 0 and the crown reaches +0.047 m. The inner violet lip sits at −0.015 m. Only the lip and short throat extend behind the host plane.

| Node | Role and dimensions |
| --- | --- |
| `riftPortal` | Placement root at the centre of the host plane. |
| `apertureAnchor` | Identity socket at the root origin for the mask and shader. |
| `portalFrame` | Continuous bezel around the 0.95 × 1.35 m ellipse. Flank inserts, bevels and emitter bands occupy separate faces on one baked material. |
| `portalInterior` | Double-sided shader receiver at local Z = −0.025 m. Its 104 perimeter positions coincide with the rear opening of the bezel. In the exported glTF, U = 0.5 + X/0.95 and V = 0.5 − Y/1.35. |

The 0.45 m recess belongs to the visual depth effect. The P3 mesh supplies a 25 mm physical setback. Keep the 0.65 m gameplay keep-out and 2 m minimum Core separation in the simulation. There is no collider or animation clip in this GLB.

The current blockout builds a circular stencil with a 0.675 m radius. Its visible interior scales the circle in X and occupies 96% of the nominal diameter. The asset follows the nominal elliptical opening in the art and content contracts. P4 must align the stencil and analytic occlusion bypass with that ellipse before device acceptance. Both oblique views use a synthetic host surface cut to the authored ellipse, 0.3 mm behind the anchor. Its outer edge has depth to show the viewing angle. The aperture has no interior wall lining: a stencil removes the host surface there, while a thick white modeled cut face would intersect the portal throat. Only room-side studio lights illuminate the wall views. The open-aperture view hides the removable shader disk. A scanned wall and live depth image have tracking and sampling error that this fixture does not exercise.

## Construction and verification

One cross-section runs around the frame. The low shell has 3,952 triangles and its disk has 104, for 4,056 total. The high shell has 9,728 triangles and its disk has 256. Both editions place additional angular samples across the six narrow panel joints. The frame uses separate physical machining coordinates and a final UV0 atlas containing four non-overlapping quadrant strips. The shader disk has independent planar UV0. The full export uses 11,025 glTF vertices, two materials, two mesh primitives, one socket and zero bones. It is 4,869,036 bytes including fallbacks. The five compressed textures total 1,841,794 bytes, with 6,990,640 bytes of UASTC blocks across all mip levels before Zstandard compression.

Cycles projects unlit color, tangent normal, packed ORM and emissive from the higher-tessellation frame onto the final triangulated receiver. It applies 12-sample contact AO over 26 mm on the assembled shell. The disk stays outside the projection. The source script checks the receiver coordinates, topology, normals and UV hash after baking. It records each source PNG hash and surface samples. The static vortex uses a fifth 1024² sRGB emissive texture shared between the two source scenes. Four 1024² PBR maps describe the frame. The self-contained GLB embeds each PNG fallback and its UASTC/Zstandard KTX2 with eleven mip levels. The linear ORM channels are occlusion, roughness and metalness.

The re-import check measures the shared rim/disk boundary, anchor pose, shader UV mapping, ground and ceiling rotations, metre bounds, emitted mesh counts, fallback hashes and decoded KTX2 properties. The repository model check covers coincident faces, planar normals, tangent/UV validity, exact material roles, decoded texture samples and structural budgets. Fixed EEVEE high/low pairs use the same cameras and lighting. The low review uses the re-imported GLB with decoded KTX2 colors. Independent score and owner review belong to P3-T7. The runtime shader, masking and Quest MR acceptance belong to P4/P5.

## Reproduce

Run from the repo root with Blender 5.2.2 LTS and KTX-Software 4.4.2. Use a fresh output directory on each run. Two source runs on the same Linux x86-64 CPU backend should match the PNGs, receiver measurements, glTF, binary geometry and packaged GLB byte-for-byte. The recorded blend hashes identify the retained packed archives.

```sh
stage=$(mktemp -d)
models=$(mktemp -d)
"$BLENDER" -b -t 2 --factory-startup --python-exit-code 1 -P blender/rift_portal.py -- --out "$stage"
python3 -B blender/package_rift_portal.py --stage "$stage" --models "$models" --ktx "$KTX"
python3 -B scripts/validate_glbs.py --manifest "$models/manifest.json" --ktx "$KTX"
asset="$models/rift-portal"
"$BLENDER" -b -t 2 --factory-startup --python-exit-code 1 -P blender/verify_rift_portal.py -- --glb "$asset/rift-portal.glb" --report "$asset/reimport.json" --ktx "$KTX"
"$BLENDER" -b -t 2 --factory-startup --python-exit-code 1 -P blender/review_rift_portal.py -- --source "$asset/high.blend" --label high --out "$asset/review"
"$BLENDER" -b -t 2 --factory-startup --python-exit-code 1 -P blender/review_rift_portal.py -- --source "$asset/rift-portal.glb" --label low --out "$asset/review" --ktx "$KTX"
```
